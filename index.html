ENGINEERED FOR WINDOWS

Pure Music Experience. Zero Clutter.

Everything you need from a modern lyrics companion, built into a lightweight native desktop overlay.

Native Windows GSMTC

Connects directly to the Windows WinRT System Media Transport API. No browser extensions, token configurations, or login accounts required.

Top Dynamic Island

Docks flush against the top edge of your monitor. Title bar completely dissolves, giving you pure synchronized lyrics with zero distractions.

Ghost Click-Through

Play games, write code, or browse the web while lyrics float over your apps. Mouse clicks pass through seamlessly without interruption.

LRCLIB Synced Engine

Synchronizes millisecond timestamps with smart YouTube video title cleaning, instrumental break indicators, and instant local caching.

Hidden from Taskbar

Lives in your System Tray notification area. Keeps your taskbar clean with quick right-click mode switching, playback controls, and toggles.

Transparent Glass Mode

Toggle between solid frosted glass card and pure floating transparent text that blends directly over your wallpaper or active applications.

CUSTOMIZABLE VIEWS

Four Tailored Display Modes

Switch seamlessly based on what you are doing — gaming, working, or singing along.
